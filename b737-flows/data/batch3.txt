== B | Takeoff Data calculation (EFB OPT) | - | 63
N Two independent calculations are required when using EFB. This means that each pilot shall perform the whole calculation instead of a single calculation crosschecked by the other crew member, both pilot shall be crosscheck using compare calculation (OPT Performance Take-off page).
X Both Pilots will:
V Follow EFB OPT Operational Procedure for Takeoff Data calculation.
X When make OPT calculation use next modes:
V THRUST RATING OPTIMUM (if applicable) choose when normal environment conditions or select TO RTG/WINDSHEAR RTG to mitigate weather influence
V FLAP CONFIG choose OPTIMUM when normal environment conditions or FLAP CONFIG 1/5//10/15/25 when TORA is a factor or rough RW or Windshear is expected
N For takeoffs, when conditions permit, consider using larger flap settings to provide shorter takeoff distance. Larger flap settings also provide greater tail clearance.
N Flaps 1 and 5 takeoffs have the least clearance. Consider using a larger flap setting for takeoffs and landing at light gross weights.
V IMCLB CONFIG choose NONE OR FIXED when normal environment conditions or OPTIMUM when during takeoff TOW In doubt, (there is high temperature, high aerodrome elevation, specific climb gradient during SID is required or combination of this factors)
N If used IMCLB CONFIG OPTIMUM, note to flight log as remark “Takeoff is performed at ImCLB OPTIMUM and airport name”.
X Remember that use of IMCLB CONFIG OPTIMUM mode leads to higher V1/Vr/V2 speeds and increase wheels workload!
V Use FMC PERF INIT page 1/2, ZFW entry from OPT W/B calculation or Loadsheet
V Ensure that N1 Thrust target, flap position, V1, VR, V2 on TAKEOFF REF page 1/2 correspond to EFB OPT calculations or applicable RW analysis
V Ensure when using reduced takeoff thrust (ATM) and Fixed Derate Thrust with ATM do not enter on N1 Limit page SEL Temperature more than 55 °C (NG), 50 °C (CL).
V Verify ZFW from the EFB or loadsheet, enter ZFW line on CDU Performance page and push EXEC key.
V Select TAKEOFF REF 1/2 page: enter TO flaps into FLAPS line and MACTOW into CG line, crosscheck FMC and OPT calculation stabilizer takeoff trim setting .
V Enter Derate or/and SEL TEMP for takeoff and takeoff speeds in FMC which was calculated in OPT. Do not enter on N1 Limit page SEL Temperature more than 55 °C (NG), 50 °C (CL).
V Check CLB thrust on CLB page CDU and verify value should be lees than R-TO thrust. If more than R-TO choose CLB-1 or CLB-2.
N Takeoff Stabilizer Trim Setting depends on TOW, TO Flaps, CG % and TO Thrust. In the LOADSHEET TO STAB TRIM Setting calculation is based on TOW, TO Flaps (1&5), CG % and maximum certified TO Thrust. In the FMC TO STAB TRIM Setting calculation is based on TOW, TO Flaps, CG % and actual TO Thrust (Full Rated or Derated). If Full TO Thrust is used for Takeoff, then Loadsheet and FMC calculated TO STAB TRIM Settings should be the same. When the actual TO Thrust is reduced by Fixed Derate then the Loadsheet and FMC TO STAB TRIM Settings will be different. In this case OPT calculation data shall be used.

#PHASE push | Pushback or Towing Procedure | 2.2.22 | 65–67
@trigger Before the first movement of the aircraft (pushback or towing), the flight crew shall verify that the cabin is secure. SCCM report the information “CABIN READY FOR MOVEMENT” via Interphone and after receiving confirmation from C or F/O “CABIN READY FOR MOVEMENT CONFIRM” first movement can be done.
@intro If jumpseat is occupied, both pilots must check and be sure that jumpseat occupant use seatbelts and all personal belongings are taken away before commencement of the aircraft movement.
@intro Flight crew shall only request engine start and/or pushback clearance after confirmation that Ground staff is ready for pushback
@intro The Engine Start procedure may be done during pushback or towing.
@intro Do not accomplish a crossbleed start during pushback.
@who Captain / F/O по колонкам документа
@callout CFO | CABIN READY FOR MOVEMENT CONFIRM
== - | Cautions | - | 66
C Do not hold or turn the nose wheel steering wheel during pushback or towing. This can damage the nose gear or the tow bar.
C Do not use airplane brakes to stop the airplane during pushback or towing. This can damage the nose gear or the tow bar.
C When operating on slippery surface, engine start during push back is not recommended due to risk of undesirable aircraft/tow tractor drift. Delay engine start until the end of the push back, setting the Parking Brake and tow bar disconnection.

== - | Starter duty cycle | - | 68
X Starter duty cycle:
V Do not exceed 2 minutes during each start attempt
[NG] V (NG) A minimum of 10 seconds is needed between start attempts
[CL] V (CL) A minimum of 20 seconds is needed between the first and second start attempts
[CL] V (CL) A minimum of three minutes is needed between the second and subsequent start attempts.
== - | Normal engine start considerations | - | 68–69
X Normal engine start considerations:
V do not move an engine start lever to idle early, a hot start can occur
V keep a hand on the engine start lever while monitoring RPM, EGT and fuel flow until stable
V if fuel is shutoff accidentally (by closing the engine start lever) do not reopen the engine start lever in an attempt to restart the engine
V failure of the ENGINE START switch to stay in GRD until the starter cutout RPM can cause a hot start. Do not re-engage the ENGINE START switch until engine RPM is below 20% N2.
V During cold weather starts, oil pressure may temporary exceed the green band or may not show any increase until oil temperature rises. No indication of oil pressure by the time idle RPM is achieved requires an immediate engine shutdown. At low ambient temperatures, a temporary high oil pressure above the green band may be tolerated.
V When starting the engines in tailwind conditions, Boeing recommends making a normal start. Expect a longer cranking time to ensure N1 is rotating in the correct direction before moving the start lever. A higher than normal EGT should be expected, yet the same limits and procedures apply.
== - | ABORTED ENGINE START | - | 69
X Do the ABORTED ENGINE START checklist for one or more of the following abort start conditions:
V the N1 or N2 does not increase or increases very slowly after the EGT increases
V there is no oil pressure indication by the time that the engine is stable at idle
V the EGT does not increase by 10 seconds (CL), 15 seconds (NG) after the engine start lever is moved to IDLE
V the EGT quickly nears or exceeds the start limit (725ºC)
X Advise Ground staff to disconnect for a visual signal after both engines are stable at idle and push back is completed.
