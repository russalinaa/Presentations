== - | Normal Refueling | - | 29
[CL] H For CL only
[CL] X When a full fuel load is required, the fuel shutoff system closes the fuelling valves automatically when the tanks are full. When a partial fuel load is required, the fuel quantity indicators are monitored and the fuelling valves are closed by manually positioning the fuelling valve switches to CLOSED when the desired fuel quantity is aboard the airplane.
[NG] H For NG only
[NG] X The RH tank fuels slightly faster than the left-hand (LH) tank.
[NG] X Fuel the RH tank part way, approximately 80%, and then wait approximately 6 to 7 minutes to let the cold and warm fuel mix enough for the indication to be more representative of the actual fuel quantity in the tank. During this time, the LH tank may be fueled while waiting for the fuel to mix in the RH tank. The same procedure is needed for LH tank to let the cold and warm fuel mix enough.
[NG] X To prevent fuel spilling follow next steps:
[NG] X 1. Do not use the Manual Override button unless there is a known FQIS issue, and fuel quantity is detected using other means (fuel measuring sticks). a. There is a possibility of the button sticking.
[NG] X 2. If a refuel gauge starts to flash, urgently ask to release the "deadman" switch immediately to prevent a fuel spill. a. The flashing gauge indicates a tank which is volumetrically full as calculated by the computer. b. The refuel valve can fail to close or the float switch can fail to open resulting in over filling the tank and eventually a fuel spill.
[NG] X 3. Make sure that refuel pressure is between 35 psi and 55 psi.
[NG] N If there is fuel temperature stratification (cold fuel at the compensator and warm fuel added on top) it is possible that the gauge will not flash before a fuel spill occurs.
[NG] N If required amount of fuel for flight is less than 19500 kg, fuel filling to each wing tanks 3700 kg is a good practice (please take into account fuel density and ambient temperature).This note protect crew from fuel spilling.

X When it becomes necessary to refuel with the APU inoperative, the aircraft battery depleted, and no external power source available, refuelling can still be accomplished:
