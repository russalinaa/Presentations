#PHASE pcap | Preflight Procedure – Captain | 2.2.14 | 49–55
@trigger Captain Call “PREFLIGHT Procedure.”
@intro The captain normally does this procedure. The first officer may do this procedure if needed.
@intro Captain will advise First Officer for light test and both pilots will observe the lights in a scan flow.
@intro After light test completed or during Scan Flow of Preflight Procedure, F/O will perform GPWS test. SP.15, Transponder Test SP.11.1 and weather radar test SP.11.1
@intro The light test is required before first leg of the flight duty. The fire warning lights shall not be checked during this test. Individual test switches or push to test features shall be used to check the lights which do not illuminate during the light test. Scan flow shall be used to verify that all other lights are flashing or illuminated. And also all system annunciator panel lights shall be verified to illuminate.
@who Captain (First Officer if needed)
@callout C | PREFLIGHT Procedure.
