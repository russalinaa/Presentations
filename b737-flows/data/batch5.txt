== C | [General] 2.2.27.1 Setting Initial Stabilized and Takeoff Thrust | - | 76
V Captain always sets initial Stabilized and Takeoff Thrust because it is his responsibility to reject takeoff
V Captain shall start stopwatch elapsed time mode (ET) before pressing TO/GA switches to record flight time
V After Takeoff Thrust is set and verify from CDU Takeoff page
V Captain must take hands off thrust levers at V1
V When FO is PF callout “SET INITIAL THRUST, CHECK ENGINE STABLE” means the Captain may advance the thrust levers to set Initial Stabilized Engine Thrust
V Engine thrust is considered stabilized, when N1 difference between engine one and engine two is 5% or less, and N1 is 40% or more.
V Allow the engines to stabilize for more than 2 seconds, before advancing thrust levers to takeoff thrust, may adversely affect takeoff distance.”
V For cold weather operation minimum N1 may be 70%. Refer to Takeoff Procedure in Supplementary Procedures SP.16 Adverse Weather.

== - | [PF/PM] Takeoff configuration warning | - | 77
X Change of terminology from CAPT/FO to PF/PM
W As thrust levers are advanced an intermittent warning horn (takeoff configuration warning) sounds if the PARKING BRAKE is set, SPEEDBRAKE LEVER is not in the DOWN position, STABILIZER TRIM is not in the green band, FLAPS are not in takeoff position.
N For more causes of takeoff configuration warning refer to FCOM/Systems Description/Warning Systems.

== PF | [PF/PM] 80 knots | - | 78
X Verify 80 knots and call
K CHECK
== PM | [PF/PM] 80 knots | - | 78
K 80 KNOTS
X and when FMA Autothrottle annunciator changes to THR HOLD, call
K THROTTLE HOLD
N 1: The PM should announce passing 80 knots and the PF should verify that his airspeed indicator is in agreement. The 80 knots calls will be used as airspeed crosscheck, high/low speed regime check and incapacitation call. If the PM is late to call 80 knots then the actual airspeed will be called by any pilots and confirmed by the other pilot. Above 80 knots, relax the forward control column pressure to the neutral position.
N 2: If the decision is to reject the takeoff, the captain must clearly announce: “REJECT”, immediately start the rejected takeoff maneuver and assume control of the airplane. This callout is responsibility of the Captain only, regardless of his duty as PF or PM.
N 3: The word “REJECT” implies “I HAVE CONTROL”. There is no other verbal handover of control in case of RTO.
